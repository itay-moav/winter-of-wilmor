# The Three Faiths

All three faiths are invented. Never use real-world religious symbols, especially crosses, in art or text. Each faith answers the same question differently: **what should happen to what we love when its time ends?** That question is the heart of Malthor's story.

## The Dawn (Velmoor)

| Aspect | Canon |
|---|---|
| God | **Solenne, the Kindled Sun** |
| Creed | *"All that sets will rise again."* |
| Teaching | Death is a sunset: the soul goes "to the morning" and will rise. Grief is honoured, but clinging is a sin against the turning of the day. |
| Symbol | A **gold sun disc with 8 straight rays inside a thin ring**. Code: `TX.symbol.dawn` (canvas) and `BT.art.symbol('dawn')` (SVG). |
| Colours | White, gold, cream marble |
| Clergy | High Priestess **Aurelle** (compassionate) and **Brother Caddoc** (zealot inquisitor). Knights: the Dawn's order, of whom Kargath was the finest. |
| Holy places | The **Temple of the Dawn** (P, a 2x2 marble temple with columns, a pediment and golden doors), the Keep chapel, and wayside sun shrines in dungeons. |
| Rites | Candles lit at sunrise for the dead. Bells at dawn. A summer festival where children ride on knights' shoulders. Censers of burning **sunthistle**. |
| Funerals | Burial facing east, with a sun disc on the headstone. |
| Flaws | Conquest: the crusade against Vael-Harrow and the chiselling-out of Root carvings. Pride. Caddoc's wish to burn the Pale Hall. |
| In game | Heals and resurrects. Half price if sworn to the Dawn, double if sworn to the Pale Hour. Blessing: +50% damage vs undead, faster sunlight SP regeneration. |

## The Pale Hour (Isenmark)

| Aspect | Canon |
|---|---|
| Goddess | **Ysra, the Still Mother** |
| Creed | *"What is loved should never change."* |
| Teaching | Change is loss. The Mother "keeps" what we love in stillness: in memory, in ice, in ritual that never varies. Winter is her season and her mercy. The dissenter **Ysolde** taught that stillness is for grief to *rest in*, not a prison for the living. |
| Symbol | A **six-armed snowflake inside a ring, with an empty, frozen hourglass at its centre**. Code: `TX.symbol.pale` and `BT.art.symbol('pale')`. |
| Colours | Pale blue, silver, white, glass |
| Clergy | **Matron Sevrine** (head of the Velmoor Hall; taught Malthor the Rite of Stillness), acolytes, frost magi. Zealots (the in-game "Zealots of the Pale Hour") dance in the snow. |
| Holy places | The **Hall of the Still Mother** (H, 2x2): ice-blue stone, lancet windows, a great snowflake emblem, silver doors, icicles. Ice-pillar shrines. Malthor's tower has a Pale altar. |
| Rites | Candles that are never lit; nothing in a Pale hall may move. The **Rite of Stillwater** (cold water poured to cure stiffness and stone). The **Mother's kiss**, which stops pain. The **Rite of Stillness** is forbidden, or ought to be. |
| Funerals | **Still-lilies** laid closed on graves, and the dead's rooms kept exactly as they were. |
| Flaws | Possessiveness; inability to let go. Sevrine's pride, and later her doubt. |
| In game | Cures paralysis and stone. Small heals. Blessing: half damage from ice. Refuses rites to Dawn-sworn parties. |

## The Deep Root (Vael-Harrow, the Lower Ward)

| Aspect | Canon |
|---|---|
| God | **Vaelor, the Root-Below** |
| Creed | *"We go down, so that others may grow."* |
| Teaching | The dead go down into the roots and feed the living; a king's duty is to be buried well. Chaining the dead above ground (as Malthor did to Morvane) is the gravest sin. Hospitality is sacred: "All are welcome under the Root." |
| Symbol | A **tree whose roots curl down and around to form a ring**. Code: `TX.symbol.root` and `BT.art.symbol('root')`. |
| Colours | Bark brown, moss green, amber |
| Clergy | **Old Thessaly, the Root-Warden** (keeper of the graveyard and the crypt key). Historically, king-priests (Morvane was the last). |
| Holy places | The **Barrow of the Deep Root** (O, 2x2): an earthen mound with a turf roof, a round carved door, amber lanterns, standing stones and hanging roots. The great **Heartwood Oak** in the graveyard. Root shrines (ring-twined roots and an amber lantern) in the crypt. |
| Rites | Communing with the ancestors through the roots. Poultices of **barrow-moss** and deep earth. Seeds planted on graves. |
| Funerals | Burial at a tree's roots. Headstones carved with the tree-and-ring. |
| Flaws | Resentment of the Dawn; insularity; acceptance bordering on fatalism. |
| In game | Cheap healing and poison cures, ancestor hints, and root-water (holy water) that harms undead. Blessing: HP regeneration below ground; undead strike 25% softer. Welcomes everyone. |

## The Conflict

- **Dawn vs Pale Hour (present):** the Dawn blames the Pale Hour for the winter, correctly, since Sevrine taught the rite. Pale zealots preach the winter as a miracle. Caddoc wants their hall burned.
- **Dawn vs Root (historic):** the crusade, the conquest and the defacement of the Old Kings' tombs. The Root forgave, but has not forgotten.
- **Root vs Malthor:** Malthor bound Morvane, the last king-priest and his own teacher, in undeath. To the Root this is why the dead walk.
- **Lyra** belongs to all three: raised Dawn, a Pale Hour mother, and in the end she "goes down" to the roots.
- **Resolution:**
  - In the main ending the faiths pray together once.
  - The Concord side quest restores the old peace relics.
  - The party's sworn faith changes one ending line.

## Writing guidance

- Priests should be people, not mouthpieces. Each faith has a kind voice (Aurelle, Ysolde, Thessaly) and a hard one (Caddoc, Sevrine early on, Root resentment).
- Never let one faith be simply "right". Each creed is partly true. The game's thesis: love means letting go, and memory keeps what time takes.
