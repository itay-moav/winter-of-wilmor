# World History & Geography

## Geography of the North

- **Velmoor:** a walled city of timber, stone and brick on a river plain. It is the seat of the Dawn and the game's hub, now frozen.
  - **Districts:** the Guild Row (north street), the Temple Quarter (the Dawn's temple and the plaza), the North Ward (Malthor's tower and the Keep), the Lower Ward (the graveyard, the Root barrow and Bramwick's mill) and the South Gate.
  - **Beneath it:** sewers, then the buried city of Vael-Harrow, whose upper tombs are the Crypt of the Old Kings.
- **Isenmark:** a city of pale stone and blue glass beyond the northern mountains, the seat of the Pale Hour. It is ruled jointly by a council of High Matrons. Winters there are long and treated as holy. It is linked to Velmoor by trade, marriage (Malthor's late wife Irena) and an embassy-temple, the Hall of the Still Mother.
- **Vael-Harrow:** the old kingdom of the Deep Root, whose kings were also priests. It was conquered three centuries ago. Its capital lies beneath Velmoor, and its people survive as the "Root-folk", mostly gravekeepers, millers, herb-wives and the poor of the Lower Ward.
- **The Mountains (the Teeth):** the pass between Velmoor and Isenmark. It is closed while the winter lasts.
- **The river Wend:** flows through Velmoor. It is frozen solid now; the sewers drain into it.

## Timeline

Years are counted relative to the game's present (Y0).

| When | Event |
|---|---|
| Ancient | Vael-Harrow rises. Its king-priests are buried at the roots of a great oak, so that "the kingdom grows from its kings". The first **Heartwood Oak** grows. |
| ~Y-400 | **The Concord of Velmoor:** the Dawn (a missionary faith from the south), the Pale Hour (Isenmark) and the Deep Root (Vael-Harrow) swear peace at a trading town's fountain, each leaving a relic: the Sun Shard, the Still Hourglass and the Heartwood Seed. |
| Y-300 | **The Dawn Crusade:** Dawn zealots, the Kindled Host, take Vael-Harrow, wall it in, and build **Velmoor** over its ruins. Root carvings are chiselled over with suns. Three families hide the Concord relics: Orrin's ancestor (Dawn), Ysolde's line (Pale Hour) and Bramwick's (Root). |
| Y-300 to Y-1 | An uneasy coexistence. The Dawn is the state faith. The Root survives in the Lower Ward. Isenmark keeps an embassy-temple. **King Morvane**'s line is honoured privately by the Root-folk. |
| ~Y-40 | Young **Malthor** studies magic under the ghost of King Morvane (a Root scholar-spirit in the crypt), and later at the court. He becomes court archmage. |
| ~Y-20 | Malthor marries **Irena of Isenmark**, a Pale Hour devotee. **Lyra** is born and raised in the Dawn, while her mother takes her to light candles at the Pale Hall. |

| Y-1 | Lyra falls ill with a wasting fever. **High Priestess Aurelle** counsels acceptance. Malthor turns to **Matron Sevrine**, who teaches him the **Rite of Stillness**. |

## The Winter (how it works)

- The Rite of Stillness halts *change*, not merely heat. Snowflakes hang motionless near the tower, clocks stop, and wounds "simply stop" under the Pale Hour's kiss.
- The rite needs an anchor (Lyra's crystal) and a will (Malthor's refusal to let go). It breaks when he chooses to let the moment pass.
- Side effects:
  - Undead stir, because the dead can neither "rise" (Dawn), "stay" (Pale) nor "go down" (Root).
  - Statues half-wake.
  - Rimeberries grow on frozen bushes.
  - Harvests have failed for a year, so the city is near famine.

## Politics

- **The Crown of Velmoor** is absent from the story; the city is effectively run by the Guild, the temples and the cursed guard. A future hook is a regent hiding in the Keep's upper floors.
- **The Adventurers' Guild** is neutral and pays heroes.
- **The temples** each claim to have the cure for the winter.
- **The thieves' guild** went underground to serve the Rat King, and through him Malthor.
