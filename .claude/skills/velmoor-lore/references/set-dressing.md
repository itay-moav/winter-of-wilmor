# Set Dressing: Objects, Lights and Surfaces

Places must **look like their descriptions**. If a note says torches, there are torches; if it says water runs ankle-deep, the tunnel floors carry water; if it says lichen or algae, the walls show them. Every object below exists as a procedural texture (128x128) and may be used anywhere it makes sense. New objects: add the texture to a pack (`js/tex-*.js`), record it here, and use it in a style table or a feature `deco`.

## How dressing is chosen (`js/maps/dressing.js`, `BT.DRESS`)
- **Per style** (`DR.STYLES`): weighted wall decals, light sources (spread out, one every N cells), corridor and room floors, ceilings and random props. Generated by a separate seeded RNG after the level, so layouts and saves never change.
- **Per feature** (`deco` on a level feature in `maps.js`): `{ walls, near, props, propCount, floor }` dresses the cells within `near` steps of a note, fight or boss room with exactly what its text describes.
- **Houses** are dressed by hand: room walls/floors/ceilings, `decals` and `props` in `BT.INTERIORS`.
- **Lights** (`DR.LIGHTS`): torches, braziers, candles, hearths, amber lanterns, glowing fungi, crystals, runes, sun reliefs and windows cast coloured light that stops at walls and closed doors, flickers if it is fire, and never shines into magical darkness.

## Rules
- No crosses or real-world religious symbols in any object; faith marks use the three symbols only (`TX.symbol`). Avoid "+"-shaped joints in windows, doors, timber and tiles.
- Abandoned homes stay unlit (cold hearths, unlit candles); lived-in places and dungeons get lights.
- Hanging props attach to the ceiling (`_hang`); furniture stands against a wall (`side`), big centrepieces (millstone, pillars) stand in the middle.
