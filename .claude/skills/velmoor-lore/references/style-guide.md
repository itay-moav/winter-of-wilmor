# Style Guide

## Tone
- Melancholy fairy tale with warmth and humour: grief, love and letting go, lightened by tavern banter and Zeyla's vanity.
- Villains have reasons. Malthor is a grieving father, Morvane and Kargath are victims, and Sevrine is sincere.
- Keep in-game text short: 1-3 sentences per box. Use second person ("You find...") for narration and quoted lines for speech.

## Naming conventions by culture

| Culture | Feel | Examples | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Velmoor (Dawn) | warm, Anglo-Latin, soft | Aurelle, Caddoc, Orrin, Brannoc, Seraphine, Lyra | harsh consonant clusters |
| Isenmark (Pale Hour) | cool, sibilant, Nordic-tinged | Sevrine, Ysolde, Ysra, Irena, Isenmark | "-heim", obviously real Norse names |
| Vael-Harrow (Root) | earthy, old, double vowels and "-wick/-harrow" | Vaelor, Morvane, Thessaly, Bramwick, Vael-Harrow | anything Latinate |
| Guild / adventurers | any | Torvald, Mei, Lark, Elowen, Nyx | real celebrities |

Places follow the same pattern. Velmoor buildings have merchant names ("The Frostbitten Flagon", "The Warm Hearth Inn"). Isenmark places are "Hall of...". Root places are "Barrow", "Hollow" or "Mill".

## Content rules
- **No real-world religious symbols** (no crosses, crescents, six-pointed stars of David, om, etc.). Graves and holy art use only the three invented symbols.
- **Art:** anime/JRPG style. Female characters may wear revealing fantasy outfits but stay non-explicit (no nudity) and always clearly adult. Male characters are heroic, often muscular, sometimes shirtless.
- **Violence:** fantasy combat, no gore beyond "bones underfoot".
- **Not derivative:** no copyrighted *Bard's Tale* text, places (Skara Brae, Mangar), spell names or maps. Classic riddles that are folk tradition (footsteps, tomorrow) are fine.

## Riddles
- They must be answerable from the story or from common sense, and themed to their place.
- Accept several phrasings (singular and plural, with or without articles).
- Put a hint in a nearby note, a rumor, Zeyla's fortune or Thessaly's communion.

## Colours and UI
- **Faith colours:** Dawn `#ffd76a`, Pale Hour `#bfe4ff`, Root `#b8d27a`.
- **UI:** dark navy panels with gold borders, serif headings (Palatino or Georgia).
