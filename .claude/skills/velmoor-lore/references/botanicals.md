# Botanicals

Plants, herbs, fungi and trees of the North. Most are **lore hooks not yet in the game** (marked *hook*). The column "Game hook" suggests how each could become an item, an ingredient, a quest or set dressing. When one is implemented, change *hook* to **in game** and note the item or event id.

Each plant has a faith tie where it makes sense, because flora is part of how the three religions see the world.

| Plant | Faith tie | Description | Status / game hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heartwood Oak** | Deep Root | The great sacred oak of Vael-Harrow; Root kings were buried at its roots. A descendant stands in Velmoor's graveyard, where Lyra's locket is buried in the ending. Its seeds glow amber and beat faintly like a heart. | **In game:** the Heartwood Seed relic (`heartseed`) and the ending scene. *Hook:* a graveyard oak sprite. |
| **Sunthistle** | Dawn | A golden thistle whose head turns to follow the sun, even through cloud. Burned in Dawn censers; its smoke smells of honey and ash. Caddoc's censer is full of it. | *Hook:* a fire-resistance potion ingredient; it won't grow during the winter, so it is rare and pricey. |
| **Still-lily** | Pale Hour | A white lily that closes at dusk and never reopens once picked. It stays perfect for years. The Pale Hour lays closed still-lilies on graves. | *Hook:* a "Still-lily Draught" that freezes one enemy group for a round; set dressing in the Pale Hall. |
| **Frostbloom** | Pale Hour | A crystalline blue flower that blooms only in snow, sacred in Isenmark. It has spread wildly through Velmoor's endless winter. The Pale zealots wear it as a badge. | *Hook:* a Stillwater-rite ingredient; wilts when spring returns (a post-game visual change). |
| **Barrow-moss** | Deep Root | Soft amber-glowing moss that grows in crypts and barrows. Thessaly packs wounds with it ("poultice of the deep earth"). | Partly in game: the Root Barrow's healing service. *Hook:* a craftable salve. |
| **Gravevine** | Deep Root | A creeper that roots in graves and flowers small white stars on the anniversary of a burial. The Root-folk read it like a calendar. | *Hook:* graveyard set dressing; a riddle about anniversaries. |

| **Emberleaf** | (folk, Dawn-leaning) | A red-veined herb brewed as a warming tea in every Velmoor kitchen. Tied in folk tradition to the *Hearthfire Lay* bard song. | *Hook:* a consumable that cures a "chilled" status. |

## Rules for adding plants
- Give every plant a sensory detail (look, smell or taste) and a cultural use.
- Tie it to a faith or to the winter whenever possible.
- Respect the seasons: sunthistle and sunfall wheat can't grow in the winter, frostbloom and rimeberry thrive, and in the post-game spring the balance flips.
